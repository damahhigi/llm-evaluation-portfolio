# Customer Support Policy

## Damaged Orders

Customers who receive a damaged item should contact Customer Support.

The customer must provide:
- Their order number
- A photo of the damaged item

The policy does not guarantee that a damaged item qualifies for a refund.

## Order Cancellation

Customers may cancel an order before it has been shipped.

Once an order has been shipped, it cannot be cancelled.

## Returns

Items may be returned within 30 days of delivery.

To qualify for a return:
- The item must be unused.
- The item must be in its original packaging.

## Late Deliveries

Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days.

If an order has not arrived after 5 business days, the customer should contact Customer Support.
